About CodeWatt

Technology with purpose.

CodeWatt is a tech agency based in Chittagong, Bangladesh, bridging the gap between digital solutions and physical hardware. We build custom web development and e-commerce platforms for businesses and startups worldwide, alongside custom electronics, robotics, and smart IoT projects for students across Bangladesh.

We believe scalable technology should be accessible, practical, and built to last. Whether launching a high-converting business website or helping a student build their first working hardware prototype, we bring the same discipline, creativity, and commitment to quality to every build.

Our expertise spans two core areas. On the digital side, we design and develop modern web apps and online stores that drive real business growth. On the hardware side, we engineer Arduino, ESP32, and IoT automation projects—empowering school, college, and polytechnic students to transform academic ideas into winning prototypes for final-year projects, science fairs, and tech competitions.

We ensure that every project—digital or hardware—comes with clear technical documentation, presentation guidance, and dependable post-delivery support. From website maintenance to hardware bug fixing, we focus on getting the details right so you can rely on the final result from concept to completion.

Our mission

To empower businesses with scalable digital solutions and help students turn their ideas into working hardware prototypes — all from one passionate tech studio.
