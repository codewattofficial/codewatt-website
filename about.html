About CodeWatt

Technology with purpose.

CodeWatt is a small technology studio from Bangladesh, building modern websites, electronics, robotics and IoT solutions for businesses, startups and students across the globe.

We believe in practical, creative, and accessible technology. From a startup landing page to a student science fair project, every build gets the same attention to detail and commitment to quality.

Our mission

To empower businesses with a strong digital presence and help students turn their ideas into working prototypes — all from one passionate studio.

Our journey

From small projects to big impact

2021

CodeWatt started as a freelance web design side project in Dhaka, helping local shops get online.

2022

Expanded into Arduino and electronics, supporting university students with academic projects.

2023

Launched IoT services with smart home and sensor dashboard solutions for small businesses.

2024

Served international clients and completed over 50 projects across web and hardware.

2025

Building CodeWatt as a full-stack technology studio, still rooted in Bangladesh, reaching worldwide.
